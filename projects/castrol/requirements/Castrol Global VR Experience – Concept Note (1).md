# Castrol Global VR Experience – Concept Note
Mining Indaba 2027 · Nov 10, 2026
## Overview
The Castrol Global VR Experience is a short, hands-on virtual reality journey for the Castrol stand at Mining Indaba 2027 in South Africa. In under three minutes, visitors travel from Castrol's beginnings to the heart of a working mine. They see exactly which Castrol products protect each machine, and end with real customer results and a conversation with the Castrol team.
## Objective
Make Castrol's value in mining easy to grasp and hard to forget, and turn every VR session into a conversation with the Castrol team.

- **Engage:** draw senior decision-makers to the stand with a memorable, premium experience.

- **Educate:** show where Castrol products work inside the machines that run a mine, and why they matter.

- **Prove:** back the story with Castrol's published product benefits and customer results.

- **Convert:** hand every visitor over to the Castrol team with a clear next step.
## The concept
**Big idea:** one Castrol drop takes the visitor on a journey, from Castrol's heritage to the toughest machines on earth.

The experience opens inside the Castrol drop, where portal rings tell Castrol's story. The visitor then chooses a surface or underground mine and is taken face to face with full-size mining machines. Rings around each machine reveal the component and the Castrol product that protects it. Castrol OptiWell and real case studies then show the value for the whole operation.
## Experience flow (2 minutes 40 seconds)


| Time | Phase | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:36 | **Phase 1: Why Castrol** | Inside the Castrol drop, five rings tell Castrol's journey: heritage and brand values, where it began (trains, heavy machinery, cars and aircraft), across industries, in space, and finally mining. The visitor then chooses a surface or underground mine. |
| 0:36–1:32 | **Phase 2: Mining equipment and products** | On a miniature mine table, the visitor picks a machine and is teleported in front of it at full size. After a short introduction, three rings appear one by one, each showing a key component and the Castrol product used on it, with narration. Visitors can switch machine, switch mine or move on. |
| 1:32–1:47 | **Castrol OptiWell** | The protection spreads across the whole mine, introducing Castrol OptiWell and the value it brings to the entire operation. |
| 1:47–2:32 | **Phase 3: Case studies** | The visitor chooses one of three Castrol case studies (haul truck, face shovel or mill) and sees the challenge, the solution and the verified results. |
| 2:32–2:40 | **Closing** | The Castrol drop returns with a contact card and QR code, and the Castrol team steps in. |
The equipment covers seven surface machines (mill, excavator, surface mining truck, load haul dump, wheel loader, crusher, conveyor belts) and eight underground (mill, drilling rig, underground mining truck, load haul dump, lifts and mine shaft, smelter, conveyor belts, locomotive). The Castrol products for each machine come from the client's equipment and application sheet.
## Key features
- **Portal-based navigation:** every choice and every move happens through a glowing portal ring, so the experience feels consistent and easy to follow.

- **No physical movement:** visitors stand still and are teleported between spaces, keeping the experience comfortable.

- **Simple touch interaction:** one gesture throughout, with short prompts and auto-continue, so first-time VR users never get stuck.

- **Real-scale machines:** full-size equipment in realistic surface and underground settings.

- **Product-first storytelling:** each machine shows three components and the Castrol products that protect them.

- **Credible content:** product benefits and results come from Castrol's published information and approved case studies.

- **Comfortable visual design:** calm lighting, readable spatial UI and subtitles, designed for VR comfort.
## Target audience
Senior decision-makers and influencers in mining, most of whom are new to VR and short on time:

- Mining owners and executives

- Machine fleet owners and operators

- Heads of sales and procurement

- Heads of marketing

- Government officials, bureaucrats and regulators

## Value for Castrol
- **Stand-out presence:** an immersive experience that draws decision-makers to the Castrol stand.

- **Product visibility:** puts Castrol's mining range and its benefits in front of every visitor, machine by machine.

- **Qualified conversations:** each session ends with a hand-over to the Castrol team, with the visitor's interests already clear.

- **Measurable engagement:** analytics show which mines, machines and case studies interest visitors most.

- **Reusable asset:** the experience can be used beyond Mining Indaba, at future events, customer visits and sales meetings.
